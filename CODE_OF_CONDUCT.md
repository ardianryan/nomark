# Code of conduct

NoMark welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss technical choices with evidence, accept constructive feedback, and help keep discussions understandable. Respect privacy, attribution, and others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private documents, credentials, or personal information without permission.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

Report a concern to **me@ardianryan.com**. Include relevant links and context; avoid sharing more personal information than needed. Reports will be handled privately as far as practical. The maintainer will review the context and may request clarification.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban participants according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is unacceptable. If you believe a decision is mistaken, contact the same address with additional context.
